For the BMM "Science-per-Token" Meta-Experiment

Goal: To run a "naturalistic field experiment" on the relationship between AI usage and scientific progress, here instantiated as the projects we complete by the end of the course.
(This is deliberately underspecified, as there is no one way to operationalize progress, and one of the sub-goals here is simply to build some form of dataset that might allow us to gain some traction on how we might operationalize such progress in the future.)


* Participants in the experiment receive a credit card for AI usage ($250 to start; more possible depending on usage).
* Coco will provide an overview of tools available for "agent orchestration" (the process of using agents to facilitate work), and can answer any questions about tips and tricks for usage.
* Participants will then aggregate their AI usage data at the end of the course. 
    * This data will remain exclusive to participants, unless explicit permission is given otherwise.


Analysis Option(s):

1. "The Agentic Counterfactual": Consider the final output of your project the "verifier".
    1. Once specified as such, we can then "counterfactual simulations", asking new agents (different from the ones you used) to reproduce results, generalize, or find different routes to the "output" you produced.
    2. Reproduction can be step-by-step across the timeline of your progress, and outputs (between what you produced and what new agents produce at each step) can be compared.
2. "The Agentic Doppel-ganger":
    1. It's your project. The model hasn't (yet) trained on it. You know what route you took to get the answers you were looking for? Does another agent take the the same route when asked the same questions?


Potential Output(s):

1. A "Prospective Perspective": Towards the Collective Derivation of a Metric that Tracks the Informational ROI of Agentic AI Usage in the Scientific Process
